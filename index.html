Wenjun Fan

Assistant Professor
School of Engineering & Technology · University of Washington Tacoma

My research interests include cybersecurity, network and systems security, honeypot technologies, blockchain and peer-to-peer networking, and AI-enabled security. My work explores practical approaches to understanding threats, detecting malicious behavior, and improving the security and resilience of networked systems.

Research

My research focuses on designing and evaluating security mechanisms for networked systems. Current themes represented in my selected publications include LLM-powered adaptive honeypots, Bitcoin P2P network measurement and security, intrusion detection and threat tracing in Named Data Networking, and secure decentralized applications.
