# About Me

Welcome to my webpage! I am currently a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the University of Washington, Tacoma, USA. My research interests include cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, network softwarization, blockchain, and cloud computing.

I earned my Ph.D. in Telematics Systems Engineering from the Technical University of Madrid (UPM), Spain, in 2017. My doctoral research was conducted under the supervision of [Prof. David Fernández](https://www.dit.upm.es/~giros/en/member/david.fernandez/) in the [Department of Telematics Engineering (DIT)](https://www.dit.upm.es/). The department has played a pioneering role in developing the [first network in Spain](https://www.upm.es/internacional?id=CON24515&prefmt=articulo&fmt=detail) with the TCP/IP protocol in early autumn of 1985, providing a stimulating research environment for my doctoral studies.

Before joining the University of Washington, I was a postdoctoral associate at the University of Colorado Colorado Springs, USA (2019–2021), where I participated in the NSF CyberCorps® Scholarship for Service program ([SFS](https://sfs.opm.gov/)) at UCCS, called Colorado-Washington Security Scholars Program ([CWSSP](https://cwssp.uccs.edu/)).
Also, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Kent, Canterbury, UK (2017–2019). During this postdoctoral study, I took part in the EU Horizon 2020 [C3ISP project](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/700294), which developed four pilot use cases to investigate collaborative intelligence through the confidential sharing and analysis of cyber-threat information.
