<h2>Prospective PhD Students</h2>

<p>
My research group is continuously looking for motivated PhD students who are passionate about research and eager to explore challenging problems in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and related areas.
</p>

<p>
If you are considering pursuing a PhD under my supervision, I encourage you to get in touch. Beyond academic qualifications and technical skills, I particularly value the following qualities:
</p>

<p>
<strong>1. Motivation and a Sense of Purpose.</strong>
Know what you want to pursue, have clear goals, and be genuinely motivated to achieve them.
</p>

<p>
<strong>2. Integrity and Honesty.</strong>
Be honest, responsible, and trustworthy. In research, integrity is fundamental. Honesty is always the best policy.
</p>

<p>
<strong>3. Perseverance and Resilience.</strong>
Research is rarely a smooth journey. Be patient, learn from setbacks, and keep moving forward. As a saying attributed to Leo Tolstoy goes:
</p>

<p style="text-align:center; font-size:1.15em; font-weight:bold; color:#ffc700 !important;">
“Genius is continuous patience.”
</p>

<!--p style="text-align:center; font-style:italic;">
As a saying attributed to Leo Tolstoy goes, "Genius is continuous patience."
</p-->

<p>
If these qualities resonate with you, please do not hesitate to contact me to discuss potential PhD opportunities.
</p>

</div>



## Research Opportunities

Prospective students interested in joining my research group are welcome to contact me.

Please include the following in your email:

- A brief introduction of yourself and your academic background.
- Your research interests and potential research directions.
- Your CV or résumé.
- Your academic transcripts, if available.
